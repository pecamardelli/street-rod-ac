## Executive summary

22 bugs, 38 stability, 5 security/integrity, 13 duplication clusters (5 drifting), 11 pattern deviations, 9 performance, 14 clean-code, plus test-coverage gaps. The fixes from the 2026-09-24 audit hold: global handlers, the INI backup, the manifest ordering, the race-result pipeline and the Lua result file were all re-checked and are sound. The top three now:

1. **Every car with specs is priced at $5,000** (`Services/Catalog/CarProfileService.cs:227`). The price uses bhp/kg × 1000, which is 150–400, so the $5,000 floor catches nearly every car. The market's price ladder collapses, mid-band dealers find nothing (visible in the 2026-09-24 log), and precedence treats every car as an economy car. This has been wrong since the first commit.
2. **The solution does not build**: EngineBench has been broken since PR #20, so `rated`/`renew` can't run. Nothing builds the solution before a merge.
3. **A wrong AC path closes the game without a word** (`App.xaml.cs:282-294`), and a car mod can write outside its folder through `data.acd` entry names or `POWER_CURVE` (`CarDataOverlay.cs:588,690`).

Race-pipeline integrity also has two ways to forfeit a finished race: a file for another save is quarantined, and the file is archived before the save commits. Every race also costs an extra 30 minutes at the diner.
