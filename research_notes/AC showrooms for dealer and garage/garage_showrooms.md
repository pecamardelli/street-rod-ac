# AC showrooms that look like a home garage / workshop (for Street Rod AC's garage scene)

Scope: Assetto Corsa showroom mods (content/showroom, loaded by CM Custom Showroom / AcTools Dark renderer) that look like a private garage, tuning shop, workshop, lock-up, barn or hot-rod shop. Researched 2026-09-23. Nothing was downloaded; the looks come from listing text and search snippets, not from inspecting files.

## Which garage/workshop-style showrooms exist for AC?

### Takeaway
There are only a few garage-themed showrooms. The best known are Rob Taylor's 2017 "Garage Showroom" and "Garage 2 Showroom", both on OverTake.gg and free. Both are photo backdrops in a clean, upmarket garage style, not a gritty American workshop. Other garage-style ones are game rips (NFS 2015, NFSU2, GT4), and some of those are paid Patreon posts or ship as tracks rather than showrooms. None of them matched a 1960s–90s US hot-rod shop.

### Inferences
- The closest free fits are Rob Taylor's Garage Showroom (#1) and the Basic Day Refurbished rework with wooden garage doors (#4). Neither is a gritty US workshop.
- The garage-themed options with a stronger mood (NFS 2015, NFSU2, GT4) are rips from commercial games, and some are track-format. They are unsuitable for a shipped project.

### Gaps
- Screenshots were not inspected, so how gritty or clean #1, #4 and #8 look, and how many cars fit, is unknown.
- I found no showroom explicitly labelled "barn", "US home garage" or "hot-rod shop". Reddit and the CM Discord were not searched directly because search returned no Reddit hits.

## Which are best-regarded?

### Takeaway
Rating samples are tiny. Rob Taylor's garages are the highest-rated garage showrooms (4.78 with 9 ratings, and 5.0 with 4). Paulaob's pack is the most downloaded but draws complaints about brightness and performance.

### Cited Findings
- Garage Showroom: 4.78/5 (9), 6,283 downloads, "Probably the best showroom yet" — [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/garage-showroom.14568/)
- Garage 2: 5.0 (4), 2,437 downloads — [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/garage-2-showroom.14809/)
- Showrooms & CM Presets Pack: 4.58 (24), 40,747 downloads — [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/showrooms-and-cm-presets-pack.14786/)
- PolyRoom is billed as "one of the most famous photo showrooms for Assetto Corsa", but it is a studio, not a garage — [OverTake tag list](https://www.overtake.gg/tags/showroom/)

### Inferences
- The garage-showroom niche is small and was last active around 2017–2022. I found no garage-style showroom released in 2024–2026.

### Gaps
- There are no rating aggregates for the Patreon or lucas-mods items.

## Any explicitly "home garage", "shop", "US garage" or "barn"?

### Takeaway
Only "GT4 Home & Garage" uses the phrase "home garage", and it is a Gran Turismo 4 rip shipped as a track. No AC showroom I found is explicitly an American garage, hot-rod shop or barn. The "cosy home garage with wooden floors" backdrop belongs to AC EVO (a separate game), not to AC1.

### Cited Findings
- GT4 Home & Garage (shin956, Patreon, track format, needs CSP) — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/gt4-home-garage-121471138)
- AC EVO ships 11 backdrops, including "cosy home garage settings with wooden floors". It is not usable in AC1 — [Coach Dave Academy](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/assetto-corsa-evo-showroom-and-functions-guide/)
- Steel 04 "The Customizable" ships an FBX. ksEditor can import FBX, set up shaders and textures, and save a KN5 over the showroom file — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/62561972); [search snippet on the ksEditor workflow](https://assettocorsamods.net/threads/how-to-make-a-showroom.2981/)

### Inferences
- If the right look matters, building our own KN5 is realistic. We could take a licensed garage model (for example a CC-BY or CC0 garage on Sketchfab) and run it through ksEditor or our own converter (we already write KN5s). This avoids licensing problems with game rips.

### Gaps
- I didn't survey Sketchfab or CC0 garage models; that was outside this pass.

## Any that allow redistribution or reuse?

### Takeaway
None of the listings I read state any licence or reuse permission. Treat every one as all rights reserved. The game rips (NFS, GT4, possibly GTA) cannot legally be redistributed.

### Cited Findings
- Garage Showroom, Garage 2, Showroom Pack-1 and Paulaob's pack: no permissions text on their pages — [OverTake 1](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/garage-showroom.14568/); [OverTake 2](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/garage-2-showroom.14809/); [OverTake 3](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/assetto-corsa-showroom-pack-1-10-showroom.55240/); [OverTake 4](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/showrooms-and-cm-presets-pack.14786/)

### Inferences
- A mod can be referenced for the user's own install: detect it in `content/showroom` if present and fall back to Hangar. Bundling it would need written permission from the author (Rob Taylor would be the first to ask).
- Showroom Basic Day Refurbished is derived from Kunos's showroom. It may be usable wherever the stock Kunos showrooms are, since players already own AC, but it cannot be shipped as a standalone file.

### Gaps
- I didn't find contact or licence details for Rob Taylor, Rogerson Roller or hdend.
