# AC track/map mods with dealership, car-lot, garage, parking or workshop areas

Scope: AC (original, not EVO) track mods usable as a drive-to location or as a source of a showroom-style backdrop for Street Rod AC's garage/dealer scenes (AcTools "Dark" renderer loading a KN5). Researched 2026-09-23. No files were downloaded. Several aggregator sites (assettoworld.com, modland.net) returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher; those details are unverified. No links found dead, but aggregator mirrors (acmods.net, modland, assettocorsamods.io) are often reposts of OverTake originals and are not the authors' own pages.

## Which AC maps include dealerships, used-car lots, gas stations, parking garages or open garage interiors?

### Takeaway
Only one AC map found explicitly includes a car dealer lot: juicter6000's "Canadian Parking Lot" (a Walmart plaza with a dealer yard, WIP, 2020). Everything else is parking structures, underground garages, Japanese parking areas (Daikoku), drag strips with pits, or highway maps with gas-station meet spots. None is a finished American dealership showroom interior, so a dealer scene will most likely have to be put together from a parking lot or garage map plus props.

### Cited Findings

- **Canadian Parking Lot v1.1**, by juicter6000, free (OverTake download, login required). Released 6 Feb 2020, updated 7 Feb 2020, 144.5 MB, about 14.8k downloads, rated 3.57/5 from 7 reviews. Described as a "Work in progress Parking lot, And Dealer lot. Original Buildings." It has Tim Hortons, McDonald's and Walmart buildings and is mainly meant for drifting. Canadian (North American) setting. — [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/canadian-parking-lot.30798/); tags (buildings, dealer, lot, Walmart…) per [search index / modland mirror](https://www.modland.net/assetto-corsa-mods/tracks/canadian-parking-lot.html); [discussion thread](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/canadian-parking-lot.178420/)

### Inferences
- For a used-car-lot scene, the best candidates are Canadian Parking Lot (it actually has a dealer yard) and Driver: The Interview with its 24 parked-car pits. For a home-garage or underground mood, CrazTheKing's Parking Garage / KC Drift Garage or FZC_Underground are closest. Any of them would still need dressing: signage, price tags, lighting.
- Maps with many pit boxes (Underground Mall: 120; Driver add-on: 24; Parking Deck: 20) already have spawn points where cars can be parked in rows, which matches how the game places dealer cars.
- Drag strips like Englishtown fit a later "drag race event" location better than a dealer backdrop.
- Black Cat County is the only candidate that needs no third-party licence, because it ships with AC.

### Gaps
- I found no AC mod that is a real dealership building or showroom interior, and no gas-station-specific AC map beyond the meet spots in highway maps.
- Licences for most mods are not stated on their pages. I could not verify file sizes for Daikoku or Pacific Coast, or dates for Pacific Coast.
- The names in the "Top 10 freeroam city maps" OverTake/YouTube lists are only in the videos, not in page text. [OverTake media](https://www.overtake.gg/media/top-10-open-world-freeroam-city-maps-with-traffic-for-assetto-corsa-install-guide-may-2023.5752/)

## Are there any ports/recreations of SLRR Valo City or similar small-town American cities for AC?

### Takeaway
I found no Assetto Corsa port of Valo City. A licensed port exists for **BeamNG.drive**, and it is important because it shows ImageCode LLC (SLRR's current owner) has allowed the map assets to be reused in another game before.

### Cited Findings
- **Valo City (Alpha) for BeamNG.drive**, by Buksikutya77. v0.79, released 15 May 2023, updated 5 Jun 2023, 47.5 MB. "This map is a direct port of Valo City from the game Street Legal Racing: Redline." More than 63 km of roads, an island, city and suburbs split by a canal, highways, working gas stations, optional night lights. The author thanks "ImageCode LLC. (the current owners of SLRR) who allowed me to use the map assets for this port." No AC version is mentioned. — [BeamNG resources](https://www.beamng.com/resources/valo-city.26648/); mirror listing v0.68: [modhub.us](https://www.modhub.us/beamng-drive-mods/valo-city-v068-028x)
- Searches for "Valo City" plus Assetto Corsa on OverTake, Assetto World, acmods and generic mod portals returned no matching AC track. — [search: OverTake AC tracks](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/categories/ac-tracks.8/)
- American free-roam maps commonly recommended include LA Canyons, PCH/Pacific Coast and Mulholland Drive (an 11-mile LA road through residential and twisty sections). — [OverTake: 6 best real road maps](https://www.overtake.gg/news/6-of-the-best-real-road-maps-for-assetto-corsa.1770/)
- CSP ships a traffic tool, and 2REAL publishes traffic data for free-roam maps, so a city map can be given traffic. — [2REAL traffic mods](https://2real.ac/traffic-mods)

### Inferences
- The best route to a Valo City for AC is probably one of these: (a) convert SLRR's own Valo City geometry. The project already parses SLRR SCX/RPK for parts, and ImageCode gave BeamNG a precedent, but permission would still have to be asked for this use. (b) Ask Buksikutya77 about their converted BeamNG assets. Either way, an AC track needs collision surfaces, AI/pit data and CSP config added.
- If a ready-made map is enough, Black Cat County (bundled with AC) or an LA-area map is the nearest American free-roam option. Neither has dealers built in.

### Gaps
- I found no small-town-USA "main street" free-roam AC map with confirmed dealers or shops. Searches returned only generic freeroam lists.
- I did not confirm whether Buksikutya77's BeamNG port has any licence terms beyond the ImageCode thanks.

## What tools/techniques exist to cut a section of a track KN5 into a showroom, and what are the community norms on reuse?

### Takeaway
The usual workflow is: unpack the KN5 to FBX (Content Manager / ac_tools_cmd / KN52fbx), trim it in Blender or 3DSimED, then re-export through ksEditor or a Blender KN5 exporter. Community norms are clear: get the original author's permission before redistributing any modified or converted track, and credit them.

### Cited Findings
- Typical workflow: unpack the KN5 with Content Manager, edit the FBX in Blender, export FBX, then open it in ksEditor to convert back to KN5. 3DSimED FBX exports can come out damaged when imported into Blender. — [assettocorsamods.net thread](https://assettocorsamods.net/threads/kn5-fbx-import-edit-and-export-in-blender-problems.2073/); [3DSimED KN5 question](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/3dsimed-question-an-importing-question-for-kn5-files.173440/)
- **Blender addon: Import/Export .kn5/.ai/.csv/.ini**, by "Please Stop This", v2.8 (3 Jun 2026). Imports KN5 through ac_tools_cmd/KN52fbx.exe. Track KN5 export is experimental and needs a previously saved .fbx.ini persistence file. It also edits AI lines, borders and cameras. Blender 2.8+, rated 5 stars from 11 ratings, about 4.2k downloads, no licence stated. — [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/blender-addon-import-export-kn5-ai-csv-ini-files.35230/); [updates](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/blender-addon-import-export-kn5-ai-csv-ini-files.35230/updates)
- **Blender Tools KN5/KSANIM Exporter** on VOSAN exports Blender car and track models to KN5 and processes ksEditor persistence files. — [VOSAN](https://vosan.co/apps/blender-assetto-corsa-tools)
- Several KN5 maps can be viewed together by exporting each to FBX and importing them into ksEditor. — [assettocorsamods.net](https://assettocorsamods.net/threads/how-to-edit-and-import-tracks-into-blender.3597/)
- A Content Manager showroom folder without a KN5 falls back to "showroom lite". Custom showrooms are ordinary folders under content/showroom. — [OverTake showroom search results](https://www.overtake.gg/tags/showroom/); [Showrooms and CM presets pack](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/showrooms-and-cm-presets-pack.14786/)
- There is a precedent for turning a location into a showroom-like photo spot: "Preview as a Photomode Location" (TheNuvolari, Apr 2023) makes Kunos' preview drivable. — [OverTake showroom tag](https://www.overtake.gg/tags/showroom/)
- Norms: getting permission is "a must" when converting tracks. Distributing files someone else made without permission is illegal. Converting from other games likely breaches EULAs, and the official AC forum does not want to be associated with conversions. — [assettocorsamods.net conversion discussion](https://www.assettocorsamods.net/threads/track-conversion-to-assetto-corsa-general-discussion.1004/); [OverTake: legality of converted cars](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/the-legality-of-cars-converted-from-other-games.251638/); [OverTake track conversions](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/track-conversions.255749/)

### Inferences
- The AcTools Dark renderer loads KN5 directly, so a trimmed KN5 made with the Blender addon or ksEditor should load like any showroom. Only the visual mesh is needed, not collision, AI or pit data, which avoids the experimental "track export" pitfalls.
- A legally safer alternative to cutting and shipping a trimmed copy of someone's track: load the user's installed track KN5 at runtime and hide or cull nodes outside a bounding box. Nothing is redistributed, though the author's ToS may still matter. Otherwise, ask authors (juicter6000, CrazTheKing) for permission to ship a trimmed piece, with credit.
- Game-rip content (Driver: The Interview; any GTA/Forza ports) carries extra IP risk and is best avoided for anything shipped.

### Gaps
- I found no written, site-wide OverTake licence policy for modified tracks. The norms above come from forum threads.
- I did not verify current ksEditor availability or the Content Manager "unpack KN5" menu wording in 2026.
