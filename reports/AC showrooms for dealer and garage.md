# Build the scenes; borrow only the moods

No Assetto Corsa showroom or track matches Street Rod AC's three scenes well enough to ship. None of the candidates states a licence that allows redistribution. The best-looking dealer and garage mods are either game rips or Content Manager Shader Patch (CSP)-lit tracks, and the Dark renderer does not run CSP. The strongest route is to build three lean showroom KN5s ourselves. Each one pairs a **CC0 Poly Haven HDRI** dome (Autoshop 01 or Auto Service for the home garage, a parking HDRI for the used-car lot, a photo-studio HDRI for the indoor dealer) with a real floor and a few modelled walls, so contact shadows still work. Our existing KN5 writer from the parts converter produces the files. Third-party mods still have a use: players who already have them installed can pick them as scenes, and nothing gets bundled. For the garage, the one worth detecting is Rob Taylor's free Garage Showroom. For the dealer lot, the only map found with an actual dealer yard is juicter6000's Canadian Parking Lot. Nothing in the AC ecosystem is an American 1960s–90s hot-rod shop or used-car lot with bunting, so the SLRR look has to be built either way. The research also turned up a lead for a much bigger prize. SLRR's current owner, ImageCode, let Valo City be ported to BeamNG, which is a precedent for asking them the same about AC.

## What the Dark renderer actually needs from a showroom

A showroom is a small folder, `content/showroom/<id>/`. It holds a track-style `<id>.kn5` plus `ui/ui_showroom.json`, `preview.jpg`, and optionally a sound file ([actools ShowroomObject.cs](https://github.com/gro-ove/actools/blob/master/AcManager.Tools/Objects/ShowroomObject.cs)). Our game loads the KN5 path directly, so the rest only matters if we want CM compatibility. `DarkKn5ObjectRenderer` draws the showroom as **ordinary static geometry in the shadow and main passes**. It looks up no special mesh names, and the lighting comes from renderer properties: key light colour and brightness, the AmbientUp/AmbientDown hemisphere, CubemapAmbient, and the reflection cubemap. Nothing lighting-related is baked into the KN5 ([DarkKn5ObjectRenderer.cs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gro-ove/actools/master/AcTools.Render/Kn5SpecificForwardDark/DarkKn5ObjectRenderer.cs)). That source brings two design constraints. First, **if every showroom material is self-lit (ksDiffuse = 0, ksAmbient ≥ 1), which is the panorama trick, the renderer turns shadows off**. Second, **the flat-mirror floor is skipped whenever a showroom is loaded**, so the showroom has to bring its own floor.

Those two rules settle most of the choices below. A pure HDRI dome is cheap, but it leaves the cars floating without contact shadows. A hybrid of self-lit dome, diffuse floor disc and a couple of diffuse walls keeps the shadows while costing almost nothing to render. The same rules say each scene should ship with a small preset of renderer values, as CM does with its Custom Showroom presets ([acstuff.club](https://acstuff.club/f/d/23-content-manager-custom-showroom-presets/16)). Lights added in CM exist only in the session and are not stored in the KN5 ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/content-manager-lights.188508/)). CSP-driven scenes lose their lighting and skins entirely. That covers Steel Racing's Dealer, OMEGA's Concept Room and most modern parking maps. OMEGA's Concept Room is also described as having "very poor performance" even inside CSP ([Gumroad](https://omegastracklab.gumroad.com/l/om_concept_room)).

Size also matters for us. The code comment in `DealerLotViewport3D.cs` notes that some existing showrooms are "a few hundred MB" and take seconds to load, and the lot rebuilds its scene whenever the showroom changes. Community packs confirm the cost. Paulaob's 249.5 MB pack draws complaints of being "too overbright" and of slowdowns ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/showrooms-and-cm-presets-pack.14786/)).

## Community garage showrooms are clean, European and unlicensed

Only a handful of AC showrooms look like a garage at all, and the niche has been quiet since about 2017–2022. The best regarded is **Rob Taylor's Garage Showroom**: free, 23.3 MB, 4.78/5 from 9 ratings, 6,283 downloads, and called "probably the best showroom yet" by one reviewer. It ships no CM preset, and one user could not get it working in CM Lite ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/garage-showroom.14568/)). His **Garage 2** (9.6 MB, 5.0/4) goes the other way: a "Porsche based theme with polished marble floor". That suits a premium dealer better than a hot-rod garage ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/garage-2-showroom.14809/)). The same garage also exists as a drivable track ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/garage-showroom-track.14811/)). Rogerson Roller's **Showroom Basic Day Refurbished** reworks the stock Kunos room: advertising and photographers removed, wooden garage doors added. It is 76.3 MB and installs over `showroom_V2` ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/assetto-corsa-showroom-basic-day-refurbished.21815/)). Since it is built from Kunos's own asset, it can only ever be used from the player's install.

The moodier options are all rips from other games, so we should rule them out: a Need for Speed 2015 garage on MacedoSTI's Patreon ([Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/showroom-need-76943958)), a GT4 "Home & Garage" that ships as a CSP track ([Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/gt4-home-garage-121471138)), and the NFSU2 and neon-garage showrooms on the re-upload site lucas-mods ([lucas-mods](https://www.lucas-mods.com/2020/03/ac-nfsu2-showroom.html)). hdend's 10-room pack carries "GTA5Bimmer" branding, which hints that some of it may come from GTA, though that is unverified ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/assetto-corsa-showroom-pack-1-10-showroom.55240/)). The only "cosy home garage with wooden floors" backdrop found belongs to AC EVO, a separate game, and cannot be used in AC1 ([Coach Dave Academy](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/assetto-corsa-evo-showroom-and-functions-guide/)). None of the free OverTake listings shows any permissions text. So Rob Taylor's garage can be an optional "use it if installed" scene, and bundling it would need his written consent.

## Dealer scenes exist mostly as drift tracks, not showrooms

There is no purpose-made American new-car dealership or used-car lot for AC. For the **indoor dealer**, the only built-for-purpose candidate is **Steel Racing's "Steel Showroom 02: The Dealer" v1.1**. It holds up to 6 cars and comes with wood-floor, mural and 27-colour light variants. But it is a standard map, and its variants live in CSP `ext_config.ini`, so in the Dark renderer it will not look as designed. The Patreon page also blocked us from checking its price and licence ([Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/update-steel-02-79701050)). Steel Racing's "04 The Customizable" is more useful as a *method* than as a scene. It ships an FBX and tells users to re-export through ksEditor with "Save KN5 as Track" ([Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/steelracing/posts/release-steel-04-62561972)).

For the **used-car lot**, **juicter6000's Canadian Parking Lot v1.1** is the only map that explicitly contains a "dealer lot". It has original buildings in a North American strip mall. It is also a 144.5 MB work in progress from 2020, rated 3.57/5, and reviewers asked for better textures and lighting ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/canadian-parking-lot.30798/)). **Driver: The Interview** recreates the 1970s-US parking lot from the 1999 game, and an add-on gives it **24 pits for stationary cars**, which fits a row of lot cars. Being a recreation of another game's location, though, it carries IP risk ([OverTake parking tag](https://www.overtake.gg/tags/parking/)). CrazTheKing's **Parking Garage** is a six-level structure built in Blender from scratch. It is original work, but it depends on CSP and has repetitive wall textures ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/parking-garage.61281/)). Panorama showrooms such as Paulaob's **SR City Sky** (5.0/5, 139.2 MB) and focused_gaming's **3 HDRI Showrooms**, which include an industrial container yard, render cheaply. They give a street backdrop but no believable ground for several cars to stand on ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/sr-city-sky-showroom.14897/); [OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/3-hdri-showrooms.39390/)). Every real-world street scene found is European: Lviv, Manchester, Ulm and London ([Assetto World](https://www.assettoworld.com/track/street-showroom-by-yronata); [OverTake showroom tag](https://www.overtake.gg/tags/showroom/)).

The one legally clean third-party map is **Black Cat County**, because it is an official Kunos location and already in every player's install ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/news/additional-black-cat-county-layouts-revive-original-assetto-corsa-location.4424/)). It has no dealer in it. If we want to cut a scene out of an installed track, the lowest-risk method is to load the player's own KN5 at runtime and cull the nodes outside a bounding box, so nothing is redistributed. The community norm is firm that shipping a modified track needs the author's permission ([assettocorsamods.net](https://www.assettocorsamods.net/threads/track-conversion-to-assetto-corsa-general-discussion.1004/)).

## CC0 HDRIs plus our own KN5 writer make the scenes cheap

**Every Poly Haven asset is CC0**: usable commercially with no attribution ([Poly Haven License](https://polyhaven.com/license)). Its indoor catalogue covers the themes we need. For the **home garage**: **Autoshop 01**, an industrial garage with even fluorescent light and a skylight at 17K ([Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/autoshop_01)); **Auto Service**, a concrete workshop with warm ceiling lights and daylight through an open door, 16K, with backplates ([Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/auto_service)); and **Garage**, fluorescent light on concrete and shelving ([Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/garage)). The catalogue also lists abandoned_garage, empty_workshop, mechanics_workshop and hangar_01 ([Poly Haven API](https://api.polyhaven.com/assets?type=hdris&categories=indoor)). For the **used-car lot**: Beach Parking and Park Parking are outdoor options, and Parking Garage and Skylit Garage are covered ones ([beach_parking](https://polyhaven.com/a/beach_parking); [park_parking](https://polyhaven.com/a/park_parking); [parking_garage](https://polyhaven.com/a/parking_garage)). For the **indoor dealer**, the brown_photostudio and ferndale_studio series give a white-room showroom look ([Poly Haven API](https://api.polyhaven.com/assets?type=hdris&categories=indoor)).

For geometry, Sketchfab has CC BY 4.0 dealership and garage models, but they vary wildly. MattDoesBlender's "Garage- Dealership Showroom" is **7 million triangles** ([Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/garage-dealership-showroom-f03456bd287d4d13966157dd02d95c75)), ImperialBlue3D's "Car Dealership" is **132 triangles** in PS1 style ([Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/car-dealership-bb07901c912a43e1a5c2e9f6c74ee4c6)), and dhimasdc9's "Garage Interior" sits in the usable middle at about 116.6k triangles ([Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/garage-interior-7e58e5e59a2e4ea8a7652b2b9413056d)). CC BY only needs a credits file, but check the licence on each model page when downloading, because several were confirmed only from search snippets. BlenderKit's Royalty-Free licence permits games only when assets "can't be extracted by the users in an easy way" ([BlenderKit](https://www.blenderkit.com/docs/licenses/licensing-faq/)). Any KN5 unpacks in one click in CM, so BlenderKit RF assets and Fab Standard assets are the wrong fit.

HDRI showrooms are, in the community's own words, "a sphere cut in half with a 360º panoramic HDRI texture" ([Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2786839495)). Users complain they look washed out ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/3-hdri-showrooms.197570/)), which comes down to tone-mapping the HDR image to an 8-bit texture. Here is the recipe for each scene:

- **Dome:** a self-lit dome, 30–50 m radius, with an 8K or 4K LDR texture tone-mapped from the HDRI.
- **Floor:** a separate diffuse floor using a CC0 concrete or asphalt texture, so shadows stay on.
- **Props:** a few low-poly modelled walls and props near the cars for parallax. For the lot: chain-link fence, pennant strings, sales shack, sign pole.
- **Renderer preset:** AmbientUp/Down colours sampled from the HDRI's ceiling and floor, plus a matching key light.

We already write KN5s in the parts converter, so the pipeline can be Blender → FBX/glTF → our converter → showroom KN5, with ksEditor as the fallback for shader tuning ([Steel Racing recipe](https://www.patreon.com/steelracing/posts/release-steel-04-62561972)). Blender exporters also exist: moppius's is built for track scenes ([GitHub](https://github.com/moppius/blender-assetto-corsa-tools/blob/main/README.md)), and LKOLA's is MIT-licensed ([GitHub](https://github.com/LKOLA/blender-kn5-addon/blob/main/README.md)). Target tens of MB and well under a million triangles, since a dealer lot also renders up to about 12 cars. This is our own sizing, not a published Kunos budget.

## Conclusion

In practice, the scene problem is a lighting-preset problem more than a hunt for assets. The Dark renderer ignores whatever lighting a mod author set up, so even the best community showroom would need a hand-tuned preset. A self-built HDRI-plus-floor scene needs the same preset work, but it is licence-clean, small, and can carry SLRR-specific dressing that nobody in the AC community has modelled. The recommended order is:

1. Replace Hangar with an Autoshop 01 or Auto Service hybrid for the home garage.
2. Build a Park Parking or Beach Parking lot with fence, pennants and a sales shack for the used-car dealer.
3. Build a photo-studio room with a glossy floor for the new-car dealer.
4. Add optional detection of Rob Taylor's garages and Canadian Parking Lot in `content/showroom` and `content/tracks` for players who want them.

The longer play is a proper Valo City. The Valo City port for BeamNG exists because "ImageCode LLC (the current owners of SLRR) … allowed me to use the map assets for this port" ([BeamNG resources](https://www.beamng.com/resources/valo-city.26648/)). The project already parses SLRR's SCX/RPK meshes, so asking ImageCode for the same permission could eventually give real SLRR dealer and garage locations, not stand-ins.

What is still unverified: none of the candidates' screenshots or files were inspected. We could not see price or licence for anything on Patreon. We have not confirmed whether the Dark renderer's dynamic reflection cubemap picks up showroom walls, and a quick chrome-part test would settle that.
